# Discovery page brief

Decisions on what the Discovery page is for, made before rewriting it. Update this file when a decision changes.

Last updated: 2026-10-12

## Who it is for

People in New Mexico city government who have a problem or an idea and feel the lift of getting it moving:

- Santa Fe teams already working with us. The page confirms the work they are getting.
- Other Santa Fe department heads with projects they need to start and feel burdened by.
- City Council members who have an idea for a fix and need research and materials to back a proposal.
- People in other New Mexico cities. This is closer to cold outreach, so Santa Fe is the proof.

Not for primes, contracting officers, or procurement staff. That material belongs in `outreach/DISCOVERY-DRAFT.md` in the 59-projects repo.

## What should happen

- A reader forwards the page with: "Hey check this out, maybe we should do this."
- Some readers reach out for a conversation.
- A reader new to the idea sees discovery as the first part of a way of working that carries less risk, is more human, is tailored to what they need, and costs less in the long run. One sentence on the page, not an argument.

## What follows from that

- The page stands alone. The colleague who gets the forward has not met us.
- It shows the size of the ask up front: time only, typically 6 to 8 weeks. No price on the page.
- It reads like a peer explaining something useful, not marketing.
- Santa Fe is the proof, because readers can check it with the department next door.
- The relief and the evidence it produces come first. Risk language gets one line.
- Santa Fe readers will compare the page to their own engagement, so it describes what really happens.
- Target length: about half of the current page.

## Decided copy

- Hero (2026-10-12): "Find out what is needed before you build, buy, or propose anything."
- Sub-head (2026-10-12): "Six to eight weeks with the people closest to the problem, and you leave with evidence you can act on."
- What Discovery Is, big line (2026-10-12): "We help the people closest to the work see it clearly and know what to do next."

## Out of scope for this page

- The full agile and modular contracting argument. That may become its own page later. Source material: `research/2020-02-26-jaquith-michigan-senate-agile-budgeting.md` in the 59-projects repo, and Jeremy's 18F "Getting things done in Government" (modular contracting) deck, which is not yet saved in either repo.
- Procurement, contracting, and teaming-partner material.

## Structure

1. Hero: what discovery is and who it is for, in one sentence.
2. What discovery is: three short cards, the loop graphic, and one line on where it fits.
3. What it takes from you: one owner, time with staff, 6 to 8 weeks.
4. What you get: about four items, one of them the evidence that backs a proposal.
5. Santa Fe work first, then two other projects.
6. One call to action: a conversation.

Cut from the current page: stat band, "Why This Matters", pull-quote, procurement callout, "Why We Prototype", "Who's behind this" prose, and the four principle cards.
