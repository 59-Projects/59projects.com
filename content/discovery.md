**In practice:** The City of Santa Fe asked us to study how it coordinates special events, permits, street closures, and public safety across more than 20 departments. We're conducting 60+ interviews across nearly every department.

<!-- why-matters-split -->

Most de-risking advice begins once you've chosen to build custom software or buy.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup> We start earlier, with the questions still open.

<!-- ownership-quote-split -->

Before work starts, we look for a single, empowered, full-time product owner.<sup><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup> In large organizations, ask three people who owns a service and you may get three different answers. Discovery brings that question into the open early, when it's inexpensive to answer.

<!-- prototype-split -->

A city can't afford to break a live permitting system to learn something. It can afford to break a rough prototype of one.
