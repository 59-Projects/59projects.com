hero: "Find out what is needed before you build, buy, or propose anything."
deck: "Six to eight weeks with the people closest to the problem, and you leave with evidence you can act on."
whatDiscoveryIsLead: "We help the people closest to the work see it clearly and know what to do next."
whatDiscoveryIsCards:
  - title: "We listen for what people need"
    body: "Every interview asks what the person is trying to get done, and why."
  - title: "We look for the gaps"
    body: "The most useful findings are where two people describe the same process differently."
  - title: "We help you decide"
    body: "You leave with a plan and a clear go, adjust, or pause. Sometimes that includes new software. Sometimes a simpler change does the job."
loopsNote: "Discovery is the first stretch of any piece of work. On bigger efforts we return to it, so each round starts from what we last learned."
examples:
  - title: "Special Events Coordination"
    org: "City of Santa Fe"

We're running 60+ interviews, from the office that asked for help to police, fire, parking, IT, and the mayor's office, looking for the places where we can improve how teams work together in-person and asynchronously."
    tags: ["Discovery", "Strategy", "In progress"]
  - title: "The Many Paths Project"
    org: "Santa Fe, with The Life Link"
    body: "An application that helps outreach teams and case managers coordinate their efforts on the ground by tracking encampments, by-name records, and running case conferencing meetings. No single system sees everyone living on the street today, so the initiative aims to explore whether frontline workers will use lightweight tools to ease their workload and collectively build a shared picture of need."
    tags: ["Research", "Prototype", "Application Development", "Data Strategy", "Accessibility", "HIPAA"]
  - title: "Water Data Platform"
    org: "NM Bureau of Geology and Mineral Resources"
    body: "Two discovery projects: how geothermal data is used today, and how field hydrogeologists collect, report, and share measurements. **The findings shaped a front-end rebuild** and a plan for making the platform's data fully editable by the people who rely on it."

  - title: "Homelessness Services Coordination"
    org: "City of Santa Fe"
    body: "We ran an earlier discovery engagement with the city, before the special events work above. What we learned was behavioral: Santa Fe's providers already knew each other. They needed a safe way to coordinate, **so people experiencing homelessness get consistent support from one organization to the next.** That finding is now becoming the Many Paths Project."
    tags: ["Discovery", "Service design"]

    org: "Atrocity Crimes Advisory Group (ACA Ukraine)"
    body: "An eight-week discovery process that developed strategies for coordinating the work of eight separate organizations across multiple countries, and of the US, UK, and EU governments, on war crimes documentation. It was followed by **three months prototyping and building the coordination system** it called for."
